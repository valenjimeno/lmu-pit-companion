# Support

## Before opening an issue

1. Install the latest published version of LMU Pit Companion.
2. Restart SimHub and Le Mans Ultimate.
3. Confirm that LMU is in an active session.
4. Check the [User Guide](user-manuals/USER_GUIDE.md), especially the troubleshooting section.
5. Reproduce the problem once more and note the exact action and status shown.

## Bug reports

Use the [bug report form](https://github.com/valenjimeno/lmu-pit-companion/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) and include:

- LMU Pit Companion version;
- SimHub version;
- Le Mans Ultimate version;
- session type, car, and track;
- whether the car uses fuel or Virtual Energy;
- the action performed and the result shown;
- clear reproduction steps;
- the relevant diagnostics log, if available.

The diagnostics log is normally stored at:

```text
C:\Program Files (x86)\SimHub\Logs\LMUPitCompanion.log
```

Review logs before attaching them publicly. They may contain session, car, track, and telemetry details.

## Feature requests

Use the [feature request form](https://github.com/valenjimeno/lmu-pit-companion/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Describe the racing situation, the desired behaviour, and why existing actions or properties do not cover it.

## Scope of support

Support is offered on a best-effort basis. Donations are appreciated but do not purchase features, response times, or priority support.

Do not use public issues to report a security vulnerability. Follow the instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
